# How FMC uses AI

## What the AI does with a complaint

After you submit a complaint, FMC's AI reads the description and looks at the photo. It suggests the most suitable category, a priority (Low, Medium or High) and the department that should handle it, and writes a short summary so officers can understand the complaint quickly. The analysis runs in the background, so you do not need to keep the page open.

## AI suggestions are reviewed by people

AI suggestions help staff but do not replace them. The category you chose is kept; an administrator decides the assignment and can change the category or priority. If the AI cannot analyse a complaint, it is still handled normally by staff.

## How priority is decided

High priority is for problems that endanger people or property or cut off an essential service for many people, such as an open manhole or a burst water main. Medium is for significant problems that should be fixed within days, such as a large pothole or garbage piling up. Low is for minor or cosmetic issues.

## The civic assistant

The FMC civic assistant answers questions about reporting problems, statuses and departments using only FMC's own knowledge base, and shows which articles it used. If the knowledge base does not cover a question, the assistant says so instead of guessing.

# Reporting a civic complaint

## How to report a problem in FMC

Sign in as a citizen and choose "Report a problem". Fill in a title, a description, the location and a category, and optionally attach one photo of the problem. Submit the form and FMC records the complaint with the status Submitted. You can follow it at any time from "My complaints".

If your connection drops while submitting, it is safe to press submit again: FMC recognises the repeated submission and does not create a duplicate complaint.

## What to write in a complaint

A good complaint lets an officer act without having to ask you questions. Describe what the problem is, exactly where it is, since when it has been there, and whether it is a safety risk or affects many people. Mention nearby landmarks such as a shop, school, bus stop or house number.

The title needs 5 to 150 characters and the description 20 to 5,000 characters. The location is required (up to 300 characters).

## Choosing a category

Pick the category that best matches the problem: Roads, Garbage, Streetlights, Drainage, Water Supply, or Other for anything else. The category decides which department normally handles the complaint. If you choose a less suitable category, staff can correct it, and FMC's AI also suggests the most suitable category after you submit.

## Using the AI writing assistant

On the complaint form, "Improve with AI" turns a rough description into a clearer, better-structured complaint. It keeps your facts, never invents details, and may list information worth adding, such as how long the problem has existed. Nothing is submitted automatically: review the suggestion, edit it if needed, and submit when you are happy with it.

## Emergencies

FMC is for civic problems that the city fixes through its departments. It is not an emergency service. If there is immediate danger to people, such as fallen live electric wires, a gas leak, a fire or flooding that threatens lives, contact local emergency services first. You can report the damage in FMC afterwards.

## Your account

Citizens create their own account with a name, email address and a password of 8 to 72 characters; a phone number is optional. Officer accounts are created by administrators. If an administrator deactivates an account, it can no longer sign in.

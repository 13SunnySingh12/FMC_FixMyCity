# Photos of civic problems

## Why add a photo

A photo shows the officer the problem instead of only describing it. FMC's AI image recognition also looks at the photo to detect visible problems such as potholes, garbage accumulation, broken streetlights or waterlogging, and uses what it sees as extra input when suggesting the category, priority and department.

## Taking a useful photo

Show the whole problem with some surroundings, such as a landmark, shop sign or street name, so the location is clear. Daylight photos work best. For a pothole, include something for scale like a shoe or a car tyre. One clear photo is better than a blurry close-up.

## Staying safe and respecting privacy

Never put yourself at risk for a photo: do not stand in traffic, and never go near fallen or exposed electric wires. Try to avoid capturing people's faces and vehicle number plates.

## Photo requirements

Attach one photo per complaint in JPEG, PNG or WebP format, up to 5 MB. FMC checks the file itself, so other file types renamed to look like images are rejected.

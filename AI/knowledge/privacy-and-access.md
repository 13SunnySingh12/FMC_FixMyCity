# Privacy and who can see a complaint

## Who can see your complaint

A complaint is visible to the citizen who submitted it, to the officer it is currently assigned to, and to administrators. Other citizens cannot see it, and officers cannot see complaints that are not assigned to them. Officers and administrators can see your name and phone number so they can follow up.

## How photos are protected

Complaint photos and resolution-proof photos are stored in private cloud storage that is never publicly accessible. When someone who is allowed to see a complaint opens it, FMC creates temporary links to its photos that stop working after about 10 minutes.

## Officer notes and resolution proof

Investigation notes written by officers and resolution-proof photos are visible to the citizen who submitted the complaint, so you can see what was done.

## Searching complaints

FMC's search understands meaning, not just exact words: searching "dark street" can find a complaint about a broken streetlight. Citizens search their own complaints, officers search the complaints assigned to them, and administrators search all complaints.

# Reporting common civic issues

## Potholes and damaged roads

Say where the pothole is (which lane, nearest landmark), roughly how big and deep it is, and whether it has caused accidents or vehicle damage. Mention if it is especially dangerous, for example on a busy road, near a school, or hard to see at night. Use the Roads category.

## Garbage and waste

Say how long the garbage has not been collected, whether a public bin is overflowing, and whether waste is being dumped or burned. Mention health risks such as dead animals, strong smells or garbage blocking a drain or road. Use the Garbage category.

## Streetlights

Give the location and, if visible, the number painted on the pole. Say how many lights are out, whether they are off or flickering, and since when. Dark streets are a safety issue, so mention if the area is busy at night. If wires are exposed or a pole has fallen, keep away and contact emergency services first. Use the Streetlights category.

## Drains, sewage and waterlogging

Describe whether a drain is blocked, overflowing or broken, whether sewage is involved, and how deep the standing water is. Mention if homes, shops or a road are flooded, and if water stays long after rain. Use the Drainage category.

## Water supply problems

Say whether there is no water, low pressure, a leak, a burst pipe or dirty water, since when, and roughly how many households are affected. For a leak, describe exactly where water is escaping. Use the Water Supply category.

## Other civic issues

Use the Other category for problems that do not fit elsewhere, such as broken footpaths, fallen trees, damaged public benches or signs. Describe the problem, where it is and any safety risk.

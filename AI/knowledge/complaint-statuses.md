# Complaint statuses and the timeline

## The complaint timeline

Every complaint moves through the statuses Submitted, Assigned, In Progress, Resolved and Closed. Each change is added to the complaint's timeline with the date, the person who made the change and a short note, so you can always see what happened and when.

## What "Submitted" means

Submitted means FMC has recorded the complaint and it is waiting for an administrator to assign it to an officer. A complaint also returns to Submitted when it is moved to a different department and is waiting for an officer there.

## What "Assigned" means

Assigned means a specific officer in the responsible department is now responsible for the complaint. The officer reviews the details, photo, location and AI analysis, and may add investigation notes before starting work.

## What "In Progress" means

In Progress means the assigned officer has started working on the problem. During this stage the officer records investigation notes and uploads resolution-proof photos of the completed work.

## What "Resolved" means

Resolved means the officer has marked the problem as fixed. An officer can only do this after adding at least one investigation note and uploading at least one resolution-proof photo during the current round of work. When your complaint is resolved, check the proof and either give feedback or reopen the complaint if the problem is still there.

## What "Closed" means

Closed is the final status. A complaint closes when the citizen gives feedback on the resolution, or when an administrator closes a resolved complaint. A closed complaint cannot be reopened; if the problem comes back later, submit a new complaint and mention the earlier one.

## Complaint history

"My complaints" lists every complaint you have submitted with its current status, newest first. Open any complaint to see its route log (the full timeline), photos, officer notes and resolution proof.
